Weave

Survey Creator

Turn rough requirements into SurveySparrow-ready surveys.

Best for
Demo surveys, workshop surveys, vertical-specific flows, structured imports.
Primary users
SEs, Presales, Implementation, CSMs.
Open Weave
What it does
Creates SurveySparrow-ready survey drafts from structured prompts, pasted requirements, or formatted text.
When to use
When you need a demo survey quickly — especially for a prospect-specific use case or vertical.
Inputs
Survey objective, audience, question structure, variables, survey type, language, welcome/thank-you text.
Output
A structured SurveySparrow-ready survey draft or importer-friendly format.
Example
Paste a customer requirement, format it with the helper, validate, then create the survey draft.

Plumage

Response Generator

Generate realistic demo responses that make dashboards come alive.

Best for
NPS, CSAT, CX, EX, CogniVue, dashboard storytelling.
Primary users
SEs, Presales, Implementation, CSMs.
Open Plumage
What it does
Generates realistic response data for SurveySparrow surveys using personas, sentiment mix, geography, language, and response rules.
When to use
When a demo dashboard would look weak with empty data.
Inputs
Survey, persona profile, number of responses, sentiment mix, region/language, custom variables.
Output
Realistic responses that populate dashboards, CogniVue, NPS/CSAT views, and demo stories.
Example
Create a healthcare NPS survey in Weave, then use Plumage to generate 300 realistic responses.

Quill

Order Form Generator

Create polished service order forms with readiness checks and export options.

Best for
Draft PDFs, fillable PDFs, final PDFs, editable DOCX, commercial document QA.
Primary users
Sales, RevOps, Presales, Deal Desk.
Open Quill
What it does
Generates polished SurveySparrow service order forms with readiness checks, commercial details, signatures, PO information, and export modes.
When to use
When a deal needs a clean order form or an internal review-ready commercial document.
Inputs
Customer details, service line items, currency, billing/shipping info, subscription details, PO requirement, signatures.
Output
Draft PDF, Fillable PDF, Final PDF, and Editable DOCX.
Example
Enter customer and pricing details, review readiness warnings, export a customer-fillable order form.

Nest

Account Explorer

Explore active accounts and find the right customer story faster.

Best for
Account segmentation, GTM analysis, customer examples, CSM/account views.
Primary users
GTM leadership, RevOps, CSMs, SEs.
Open Nest
What it does
Lets teams explore active SurveySparrow accounts through filters, metrics, account attributes, and GTM segmentation.
When to use
When you need customer examples, account insights, region/product segmentation, or account-led stories.
Inputs
Account data with filters like industry, region, product, plan, CSM, ACV slab, billing, customer age, and status.
Output
Filtered account views, distribution charts, account summaries, and exportable account data.
Example
Find active healthcare customers in North America on CX products at mid-market ACV to support a demo story.
